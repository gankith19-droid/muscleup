DATA LICENSE - MuscleUp food database
======================================

This file covers the bundled food data in this repository
(foods_db.txt / foods_db.parquet). It does NOT cover the app code
(diet_app.py), which is a separate work.

The bundled food database combines data from three sources:

1. Open Food Facts (India and worldwide)
   Source: https://openfoodfacts.org
   License: Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0
            http://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1.0/
   Data (c) Open Food Facts contributors.

2. USDA FoodData Central
   Source: https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/
   License: Public domain (CC0 1.0). Not copyrighted.
   Suggested citation: U.S. Department of Agriculture, Agricultural
   Research Service. FoodData Central, 2019. fdc.nal.usda.gov.

3. Indian Food Composition Tables 2017 (IFCT 2017)
   Published by: ICMR-National Institute of Nutrition,
   Indian Council of Medical Research, Hyderabad, India.
   Values collected via public community transcriptions on GitHub.
   Credited here with attribution.

License of the combined database
--------------------------------
Because the combined database contains Open Food Facts data, the
combined database (foods_db.txt / foods_db.parquet) is made available
under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0:
http://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1.0/

Any rights in individual contents of the database are licensed under
the Database Contents License:
http://opendatacommons.org/licenses/dbcl/1.0/

In plain words: you may copy, use, and build on this food database.
If you publicly share an adapted version of it, you must credit
Open Food Facts and offer your adapted database under the ODbL too.

The app itself only displays this data with attribution.
